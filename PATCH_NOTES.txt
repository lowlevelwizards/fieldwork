AI 3.1G — Persistent Actor Obligations

- Important world problems now remain owned by an actor across individual action changes instead of disappearing when one atomic action ends.
- Required hostile contact can interrupt unchanged mission travel.
- Self-aid needs persist as immediate survival obligations until treatment is no longer required.
- Actors with unrelated mission roles may still fulfill separately staffed contact or casualty-security obligations.
- Existing casualty, objective, warning, and campaign procedures remain the narrow multi-step executors; 3.1H will address combat/care closure and anti-loop behavior.

AI 3.1E–F — Spatial Intent Fields & Concurrent Concern Staffing

- Team concerns now receive stable capability-based actor staffing.
- Ordinary security and responsibility movement can target an acceptable field instead of one exact point.
- Live actors without a procedural owner can directly fulfill secondary contact and casualty-security concerns.
- Legacy procedure actions now identify the concern and desired effect they serve during migration.
- Finite cover, casualty reach, and physical interaction coordinates remain exact hard affordances.
- Rejected cover-slot reservations are cleaned immediately after actor-plan arbitration.

AI 3.1C–D — Unified Actor Brain & Single Execution Gateway

- One actor brain now collects and compares every AI V2 physical action proposal.
- The action arbiter is the only module allowed to start scheduler actions.
- Cross-type replanning compares candidate utility with incumbent continuation utility and switch cost.
- Existing travel, role, defensive, contact, initiative, tactical, autonomy, and report systems now use compatibility proposal adapters.
- Compatible locomotion, attention, weapon, communication, stance, and hands actions can coexist as one actor plan.
- Added plan diagnostics, concurrent concern visibility, claim cleanup, casualty guards, and staged-withdrawal continuity.

FIELDWORK 2.7 — CONTINUOUS TACTICAL DELIBERATION

This build adds a persistent actor-level tactical reasoning layer beneath mission procedures. Operators now combine personal perception, recent threat memory, suppression, wounds, terrain cover, teammate spacing, and current responsibility into one local tactical picture.

Highest-impact changes:
- continuous tactical pictures for every capable V2 operator
- suppression states that can interrupt exposed mission movement
- terrain-aware tactical reposition proposals through the existing arbiter
- bounded commitments and hysteresis against position oscillation
- personal-perception-gated aimed fire
- non-injuring suppression of recent approximate positions
- terrain and fallback scoring for objective approach selection
- dynamic responsibility-zone data for future fully emergent objective occupation
