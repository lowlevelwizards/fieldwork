# AI 3.1G — Persistent Actor Obligations

## Delivered

- Added `ActorObligationStore` as a durable layer between concurrent concern staffing and atomic execution.
- Added persistent staffed-concern obligations with stable IDs, authority, priority, desired effect, interruption history, and action bindings.
- Added persistent personal self-aid obligations for treatable wounds with available supplies.
- Required hostile-contact obligations now receive governing-response authority and can displace unchanged mission travel.
- Unrelated mission procedures no longer suppress direct fulfillment of separately staffed hostile-contact or casualty-security obligations.
- Bound concern fulfillment, tactical contact/treatment behavior, legacy role actions, operation travel, and self aid to obligation IDs.
- Extended unified actor plans and diagnostics with active obligation IDs and obligation state.
- Added regression coverage for action-gap persistence, self-aid persistence, procedure coexistence, hostile-contact preemption, and unified-plan obligation projection.

## Boundary

3.1G intentionally stops before combat/care closure. It does not yet add direct non-procedural buddy treatment, persistent firefight plans, pursuit/flanking, or anti-loop invalidation. Those are reserved for 3.1H after obligation continuity is proven in the live sandbox.
