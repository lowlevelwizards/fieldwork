# Fieldwork AI 2.8 — Tactical Commitments & Usable Cover

AI 2.8 adds the durable middle layer between team authority and physical action.

## Added

- `ActorTacticalCommitmentStore`: one persistent local intention per actor, keyed by commitment kind, governing responsibility, and threat track.
- Expanded tactical pictures with finite weapon readiness, reload pressure, team-cohesion context, and firing-edge evaluations.
- `FiringEdgeQueryService`: produces distinct left/right exposure choices around directional cover and rejects lanes obstructed by friendly actors or the cover source itself.
- `ReloadWeaponAction`: finite, interruptible weapon maintenance that uses the existing weapon and action-channel state.
- Bounded autonomous contact fire from personally visible hostile evidence while a governing encounter has paused the operation.
- Commitment-preserving cover acquisition, spacing, observation, fire, and reload proposals through the existing authority arbiter.

## Authority contract

Tactical commitments do not own physical channels. They retain the reason an actor is acting while atomic actions continue to compete through the arbiter. Immediate survival remains above governing encounter response, which remains above mission responsibility and local improvement.

## Stabilization

Defensive-position selection now records a commitment only when the scheduler actually grants the movement action. Rejected starts release their slot and no longer produce duplicate selection history.

## Deferred

Flanking, pursuit, assault, grenades, broad morale simulation, and unrestricted search remain outside this build.
