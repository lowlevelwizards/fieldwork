# AI 3.1H — Behavior Closure

## Delivered

- Added short-range 360-degree close awareness for opposing actors while preserving hard line-of-sight occlusion.
- Preserved completed tactical cover as a durable protected relationship instead of forgetting it on the next frame.
- Made repeated tactical-commitment reaffirmation preserve its original anchor and expiry.
- Added bounded exposed self-aid after prolonged treatment deferral, while still yielding to immediate incoming-fire reaction or an active caregiver.
- Added direct live-sandbox approach and finite treatment for staffed `carrier_or_aid_provider` casualty obligations.
- Prevented unrelated actions from masquerading as obligation fulfillment merely because they share a concern ID.
- Withheld ordinary mission-role locomotion while a separate governing contact/care obligation remains unresolved.
- Converted avoid/contest contact resolution to acceptable regions with bounded completion and reassessment cooldowns.
- Added directional-cover slot hysteresis and close-contact salience improvements.
- Corrected same-faction cross-team false positives in behavioral-truth hostile-pass metrics.
- Added focused behavior-closure regression coverage.

## Architectural result

```text
persistent obligation
→ stable local commitment
→ atomic action
→ physical progress / resolution
→ reassess only when the world changes or the commitment expires
```

3.1H deliberately leaves broader combat doctrine, morale, pursuit, and authored flanking outside this layer. Its job is to make the existing perception, care, contact, cover, obligation, and execution systems actually finish what they start.
