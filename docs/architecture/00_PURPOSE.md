# Fieldwork Purpose

## Human question

How do people remain useful, connected, and humane under pressure when necessary systems no longer reliably hold together?

## Creative purpose

Fieldwork creates a persistent conflict zone where information is incomplete, resources are limited, people depend on one another, and actions alter what becomes possible later.

The game is not primarily about dominating chaos. It is about recognizing what matters, accepting responsibility, coordinating without total control, adapting when plans fail, and preserving enough people and capacity to continue.

## Player fantasy

Be a dependable member of a small team doing necessary work in an unstable place.

Competence is expressed through:

- observation;
- preparation;
- communication;
- coordination;
- restraint;
- useful risk;
- repair and recovery;
- knowing when to persist;
- knowing when to withdraw;
- bringing people and resources home.

## Why Verdigris exists

Verdigris is a living region whose ordinary systems still exist but no longer reliably hold together. It is neither stable nor empty. Roads, clinics, farms, workshops, radio networks, communities, and local knowledge remain valuable and vulnerable.

The region matters because there is still something to preserve, organize, connect, expose, exploit, repair, or lose.

## Central conflict

The prior order can no longer reliably organize Verdigris, but life continues. Three belligerent systems are attempting to shape what replaces it.

- **Northline — structure:** occupation, command, predictable routes, visible presence, organized logistics, and imposed stability.
- **Commune — connection:** radio networks, mutual aid, civilian infrastructure, local knowledge, human supplies, and distributed resilience.
- **Freelancers — adaptation and leverage:** salvage, extraction, contracts, manufacturing, documentation, broadcasting, reputation, and profit from instability.

Each faction contains a necessary virtue and a danger when that value consumes everything else.

## Why combat exists

Combat is not the default purpose of an actor. It occurs when groups pursuing incompatible world-state changes cannot or will not resolve the obstruction through avoidance, concealment, negotiation, delay, competition, or withdrawal.

Violence is one possible method for:

- continuing a mission;
- obstructing another operation;
- protecting people or resources;
- gaining time or position;
- breaking contact;
- imposing control;
- creating or exploiting opportunity.

Combat matters because something outside the combat already mattered.

## North star

> The world is unstable. Something still matters. Someone has to go.
