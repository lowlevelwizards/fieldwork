# 2.0K — Boundaries, Challenge & Warning

## Why this layer exists

2.0J allowed teams to observe another group's activity and form cautious intent hypotheses. That created a new mission-level question:

> A credible armed presence is behaving inside an area we are responsible for. Do we make our boundary explicit before escalating?

A warning is not a generic reaction to enemy detection. It is justified by a mission-specific boundary, sufficient communicated evidence, and the absence of confirmed hostile action.

## Causal chain

```text
communicated activity evidence
→ mission boundary becomes relevant
→ warning response wins the decision ledger
→ challenge procedure
→ challenger responsibility
→ IssueWarning proposal
→ scheduler
→ directed voice execution
→ personal heard-warning memories
→ encounter evidence changes
→ await response
```

## Mission boundary

A mission boundary defines:

- The area or responsibility being protected.
- The policy that makes a challenge appropriate.
- The evidence threshold required before speaking outwardly.
- The warning message and communication limits.
- The fact that warning remains distinct from violence.

The current Northline fixture boundary requires a credible activity update about an unidentified armed presence inside the monitored southern approach.

## Decision ownership

- The mission owns the boundary and warning policy.
- The decision ledger describes whether current evidence activates the boundary.
- The response evaluator may select `Issue Warning`.
- The procedure runtime creates `Challenge Unknown Contact` and assigns a Challenger.
- The actor evaluator proposes `IssueWarning` because of that responsibility.
- The scheduler owns the action lifecycle.
- The communication executor determines who can actually hear the raised voice.
- Heard-communication memory stores what recipients believe they heard.
- Encounter assessment may use the warning as evidence, but it does not declare hostility or select a reply.

## Challenge procedure

```text
Establish Responsibilities
→ Issue Warning
→ Await Response
```

Temporary roles:

- **Challenger** — states the boundary and requests identification.
- **Primary Observer** — maintains the challenged contact sector.
- **Alternate Security** — preserves coverage of another approach.

When the warning is delivered, an explicit procedure event advances the team to `Await Response`. The warning does not repeat while the team is waiting.

## Heard-warning knowledge

Recipients gain a personal record containing:

- The warning's meaning and message.
- Approximate source direction.
- Whether it was probably directed at them.
- Confidence and time heard.
- No hidden source mission, procedure, role, or tactical intent.

Because each Commune operator in the fixture hears the same raised voice directly, the team encounter can treat the warning as available evidence. No compliance decision exists yet.

## Explicit exclusions

2.0K does not add:

- Compliance, refusal, deception, or spoken replies.
- Automatic withdrawal.
- Hostility classification.
- Target selection.
- Aiming or firing.
- Deadlines followed by automatic violence.

The slice ends with Northline waiting and the Commune aware that its presence may have been detected.
